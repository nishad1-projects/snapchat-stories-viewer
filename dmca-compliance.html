DMCA & Copyright Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026 | Effective Immediately

1. DMCA Compliance Overview

ViewSnapchatStory.com respects the intellectual property rights of content creators, public figures, and copyright owners. As a web proxy and snapchat story viewer indexing tool, we process public media streams directly from Snapchat's official Content Delivery Networks (Sc-CDN).

In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512), we will respond expeditiously to valid notices of claimed copyright infringement regarding media accessible through our snapchat viewer or snap story viewer utility.

2. How Content Indexing Works

Our platform functions as an automated indexing proxy similar to general search utilities like a public socialfinder engine or public video discovery tools such as a tiktok finder. ViewSnapchatStory.com does not host, store, or re-encode video media files on our own web servers. All media items displayed in the snapchat story viewer are fetched on-the-fly from Snapchat's public CDN servers.

3. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent and believe that content indexed via our snap story viewer infringes upon your copyright, you may submit a formal notification containing the following details:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material to be removed (including the specific Snapchat username or profile URL on viewsnapchatstory.com).
  4. Your contact information (full legal name, email address, physical address, and telephone number).
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information provided in the notification is accurate.

4. Processing & Username Blacklisting

Upon receipt of a valid DMCA notice, our legal team will process the request within 24 to 48 hours. In addition to removing access to the specific media links, we offer creators the option to permanently blacklist their username from our snapchat viewer and socialfinder lookup database.
